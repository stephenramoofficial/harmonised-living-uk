What should you compare besides price?

  • What specific outcome are you hoping to achieve?
  • Which claims are important to you?
  • What evidence exists for that specific claim?
  • Would a less expensive alternative address the same practical need?
  • What are the merchant's current warranty, returns and guarantee terms?
Don't compare only headline prices. A £500–£2,000 purchase deserves a clear reason for buying. If the desired outcome is vague, the best decision may be to research the category further before purchasing.
