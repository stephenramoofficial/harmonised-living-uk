EMF Protection: What Should You Actually Look For?

“EMF protection” covers very different ideas. Start with the source, the type of field and what you are actually trying to change.

What is EMF?

Electromagnetic fields are produced by electrical systems and wireless technologies. Different sources operate across different frequencies and strengths, so a blanket statement about “EMF” can hide important differences.

Measurement versus marketing

A measurement tells you something about a field at a particular location and under particular conditions. A product claim may instead describe a broader effect on an environment or wellbeing. Those are not interchangeable.

Practical steps

  • Identify the source you are concerned about.
  • Increase distance where practical.
  • Turn off equipment that does not need to be operating, where appropriate.
  • Measure before and after changes if measurement is relevant.
  • Be wary of claims that promise protection from disease without strong clinical evidence.

Where Somavedic fits

Somavedic markets its devices as a way to harmonise or mitigate the influence of EMF/electrosmog and describes effects on the surrounding environment. The company also publishes testing and research material. Our review separates those manufacturer claims from the broader evidence question.
