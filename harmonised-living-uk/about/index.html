About the publication

Independent buying guidance for an unusual product category.

Harmonised Living UK exists to help UK readers research Somavedic and adjacent wellness technology without confusing marketing claims with established evidence.

Editorial principles

  • Manufacturer claims are labelled as manufacturer claims.
  • We do not invent first-hand testing, testimonials, clinical outcomes or user experiences.
  • Health-related claims are treated conservatively.
  • Prices and product availability are checked against current merchant information and can change.
  • We create pages only when they serve a distinct search intent or useful decision.
