Structured Water: What Is It and What Does the Evidence Say?

Structured water is a major part of Somavedic's positioning. It is also an area where terminology can make a claim sound more established than it is.

What does “structured water” mean here?

Somavedic describes treated water as “structured” or “hexagonal” water and says its devices can alter water placed close to the device. The company's UK material describes a process involving a carafe placed within about 20 cm of a device for around 20 minutes.

Why the evidence needs careful reading

Images of water crystals or laboratory observations can demonstrate that a sample looks different under particular experimental conditions. They do not, by themselves, establish that drinking the water produces a clinically meaningful health benefit.

Questions to ask

  • What exactly was measured?
  • Were controls used?
  • Was the test independently replicated?
  • Was a health outcome measured, or only a physical property?
  • Does the evidence support the precise benefit being advertised?
Bottom line: treat “structured water” as a product concept to investigate, not as a proven treatment or guaranteed health intervention.
